“Generating safety media is easy. Proving that every model number, serial,
hazard, and stop-use instruction survived generation is the hard part.”

creative presentation can vary, but these invariants cannot.

## 0:25–0:55 — Real multimodal package

to show all 23 exact models. Play several seconds of the AI narration and call
out both the AI-voice and unaffiliated-draft disclosures. Show that the final
pixels were independently read and the MP3 was independently transcribed—not
validated from the intended script.

## 0:55–1:35 — Fail closed

Select **Action weakening** and run FactLock. The plausible instruction “use
only when supervised” should be quarantined because it permits continued use
and omits unplugging. Point to canonical value, observed evidence, channel,
and the blocking reason. Explain that missing extractor evidence also blocks;
there is no confidence threshold that can average away a critical failure.

## 1:35–2:05 — Correct and retain lineage

In **The winning moment**, play the rejected and corrected attempts. Generated
speech changed the upper endpoint from `VF54399999` to `VF5439999`, so
RecallCast retained and rejected that MP3, rebuilt the narration with
character-by-character serial enunciation, and linked the passing Genblaze

## 2:05–2:30 — Human authority and invalidation

Approve the corrected asset. Then click **Demo source update** to change the
remedy from free replacement to full refund. The approved asset becomes stale
because its contract dependency changed.

## 2:30–3:00 — Why B2 and Genblaze matter

Show the B2 object count and package proof. B2 is the system of record for
source versions, generated media, rejected attempts, transcripts, OCR,
validation, decisions, and manifests. Genblaze owns generation provenance,
B2 transfer, manifest verification, and parent-linked iteration.

Close with: “The media is the output. The evidence graph is the product.”
